🔼 HIGHER OR LOWER

📖 HOW TO PLAY

  • A card is revealed. Guess if the next card is Higher or Lower.
  • Correct guess? The sip count rises by 1 — pass sips to someone else!
  • Wrong guess? You drink the current sip count. Count resets to 1.
  • Ace = highest card (14). Ties: it's a free pass.
  • Deck reshuffles automatically when empty.

👑 KINGS CUP

📖 HOW TO PLAY

  • Players take turns drawing a card from the deck.
  • Each card value triggers a specific rule — read it aloud and follow it!
  • When a King is drawn, pour some of your drink into the King's Cup.
  • The 4th King drawn must finish the King's Cup — shame (and glory) await.
  • Game ends when all 4 Kings have been drawn.

⚡ IRISH SNAP

📖 HOW TO PLAY

  • Cards auto-deal while a counter counts Ace → 2 → 3 → … → King, then loops.
  • The Spoken Count is shown above the card each round.
  • If the card's value matches the Spoken Count — hit SNAP!
  • Snap correctly: Pass 3 sips to someone else!
  • Snap wrong (no match): Drink 2 sips!
  • Miss a match (next card dealt before you snap): Drink 2 sips!

🃏 BLACKJACK BLITZ

📖 HOW TO PLAY

  • You're dealt 2 cards. Get as close to 21 without going over!
  • Number cards = face value. J / Q / K = 10. Ace = 11 (or 1 to avoid bust).
  • Hit: Draw another card. Stay: Lock in your score.
  • Bust (over 21): You drink 3 sips!
  • Stay at 21 or under: Give out (21 − your score) sips to others!
  • Perfect 21: Give out 5 sips — legendary hand!

📖 The Drinker's Deck – Game Guide

The Drinker's Deck is a premium card game collection featuring multiple themed mini-games in a single app. Pick a game mode and start the party!

How to Play

  1. Choose a game from the dashboard — each card reveals a different experience.
  2. Enter player names and configure optional settings.
  3. Follow the on-screen prompts for each round.
  4. Award points or enforce penalties as directed by the card.
  5. Play through as many rounds as you like — use the back button to switch decks.

Included Games

  • 🔼 Higher or Lower — Guess the next card and pass sips.
  • 👑 Kings Cup — Draw a card and trigger the rule.
  • ⚡ The Gauntlet — Rapid-fire Truth or Drink challenges.
  • 🔴 Red or Black — Guess the colour of the next card.
  • 🔢 Odds or Evens — Guess if the next card is odd or even.
  • ⚡ Irish Snap — React fast! SNAP when card matches the count.
  • 🃏 Blackjack Blitz — Hit or Stay — get closest to 21 without busting.
